## Translation table (messaging)

| People say | Product can address | Must not promise |
|------------|---------------------|------------------|
| Stuck in traffic again | Prioritize incidents for responders | Eliminate traffic jams |
| Road floods every monsoon | Review recurring locations in one queue | Prevent waterlogging |
| Nobody takes responsibility | Show assigned / disputed / unresolved + evidence | Prove which agency is “at fault” |
| I reported it — what next? | Decision + audit trail | Every complaint gets resolved |
| Agencies blame each other | Keep CONFLICT null until human review | AI always knows the owner |
| How do I get home safely? | Support teams coordinating response | Safe-route / flood-depth app |

**Narrative bridge:** A flooded road disrupts someone’s life. DisputePoint helps the response team coordinate what happens next.
